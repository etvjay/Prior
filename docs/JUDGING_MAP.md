# PRIOR Judging Map

## Innovation — 20%

- RFT preserves belief, reference, outcome, and calibration as a durable object.
- Circuit is persistent bounded intent across rolling Event Contracts.
- Explicit ABSTAIN is a first-class accountable decision, not a missing trade.

## Technical Implementation — 25%

- Live Shannon testnet evidence.
- DreamDEX binary Event Contract integration through the pinned Markets SDK.
- Guided owner authorization with exact bounded proposals.
- Fixed-point spend reconciliation.
- Two real BTC 5m markets under one unchanged Circuit.
- Restart/recovery evidence keyed by `circuitId × marketId`.

## UX — 20%

- Market and Forecast are distinct visual objects.
- Commitment is a visible state boundary.
- Circuit page tells the stable-intent/changing-market story.
- Proof surface exposes evidence without pretending to be a generic exchange dashboard.

## Business / Ecosystem — 20%

- Persistent decision systems can operate across rolling Event Contracts.
- The model supports humans and agents while keeping owner authority bounded.
- Forecast calibration, policy behavior, and execution outcomes remain separately inspectable.
- PRIOR composes DreamDEX’s Event Contract primitive rather than replacing its market/resolution authority.

## Presentation — 15%

- Two-minute thesis: preserve belief before reality.
- Show both legitimate abstention and one legitimate losing execution.
- Use [`docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`](DEMO_SCRIPT.md), [`docs/PROOF_INDEX.md`](PROOF_INDEX.md), and machine-readable evidence.

## Claim boundary

Proven: guided economic lifecycle, two-market BTC 5m continuity, two scored RFTs, deterministic abstention, fixed-point accounting, and restart evidence.

Not proven: autonomous execution, production-safe executor security, DreamDEX-level exactly-once semantics, and production daemon reliability.
